# 🏥 Day-03; Hospital Patient Details Analysis

## 📌 Business Overview & Objective; this analysis explores Treatment Outcome, Admission Details, various Departments.

## 📊 Key KPIs; 
* **Total patients :** 4k
* **Average Bill:** 127.55k
* **Average Length Of Stay:** 10.55
* **Recovery Rate:** 0.72

## 💡Key Insights;
### Visual 1: Admission Type Distribution (PIE CHART)
* The pie chart shows that emergency, elective and outpatient admissions are almost evenly distributed. Emergency admissions account for the largest share at approximately **33.86%**. 
* but the difference between the three categories is small.
### Visual 2; Treatment Outcome Distribution (FUNNEL CHART)
* The funnel shows that recovered patients form the largest outcome group, representing about **72%** of all patients. 
* The remaining patients are distributed among those still under treatment, referred patients and patients discharged against advice.
* Despite Highest recovory, referred Emergencies need urgent attention to **reduce transport-related risks**.
### Visual 3; Patients Admission Strength (AREA CHART)
* both 2023 & 2024 peaked in july, an unusual trend;
* however 2024 recorded more admissions overall compared with 2023, indicating higher patient volume.
### Visual 4; Department Distribution (column chart)
* Cardiology has the highest patient count at **469**, while Pediatrics has the lowest at **442**. 
* However the difference is relatively small, indicating that patient volume is fairly evenly distributed across departments.
