# ⚽ Day-01; Foot Ball Match Analysis

## 📌 Business Overview & Objective; this analysis explores football match outcomes, team scoring performance.

## 📊 Key KPIs; 
* **Total matches:** 4k
* **Total goals:** 19k
* **Average of total goals:** 5.10
* **Average attendance:** 34.07k

## 💡Key Insights;
### Visual 1: Matches by weather (funnel chart)
* **Clear weather** recorded the highest number of matches at **994**, while **rainy conditions** recorded the lowest at **909**. 
*The distribution is relatively balanced, indicating that the dataset is not heavily concentrated in one weather condition.
### Visual 2; Match results distribution (pie chart)
* **Home wins** account for **1.59k** matches and **away wins** for **1.55k**, while **0.66k** matches ended in **draws**. 
* Therefore, decisive matches substantially outnumber draws in this dataset, with home and away wins occurring in relatively similar numbers.
### Visual 3; Monthly Goal-Scoring Trend (line chart)
* The monthly trend shows that scoring levels fluctuate over time rather than remaining constant.
* Several months record noticeably higher or lower total goals, indicating variation in overall scoring intensity across the competition period.
### Visual 4; Total goals by league (column chart)
* The three leagues have very similar total goal volumes. **Championship League** records **6,499** goals, **Premier Division** **6,495** and **Cup Series** **6,404**. 
* This indicates that scoring is broadly consistent across the three leagues rather than being concentrated in one competition.
### Visual 5; Fouls, Yellow Cards & Red Cards (pie chart)
* Fouls Committed are substantially more frequent than disciplinary cards, with **55k fouls** compared with **11k yellow cards** and **2k red cards** across the dataset. 
* This indicates that only a portion of recorded fouls resulted in a yellow or red card.
