# 🎵 Day-05; Spotify Streaming Analysis 

## 📌 Business Overview & Objective; this analysis explores Subscription Types, Genre types Listening by the People, Streaming Trend Over the Year, Type of Devise People Listening From.

## 📊 Key KPIs; 
* **Total Streams:** 4k
* **Total Listening Hours:** 212.38
* **Skip Rate:** 0.24
* **Average Popularity:** 50.74

## 💡Key Insights;
### Visual 1: Streaming Activity Across Subscription Types (funnel chart)
* Free users generated the highest streaming activity with **1,654 streams**, followed by Premium Individual users with **1,445**. 
* Premium Student recorded the lowest activity at 430 streams
### Visual 2; Streaming Activity by Music Genre (pie chart)
* Bollywood generated the highest number of streams with **439**, closely followed by Indie with **438**, while Rock recorded the lowest with **398** streams. 
* Overall, streaming activity was relatively evenly distributed across genres rather than being dominated by a single genre.
### Visual 3; Monthly Goal-Scoring Trend (line chart)
* Streaming activity fluctuated month-to-month rather than following a consistently increasing or decreasing pattern.
* August 2023 recorded the highest monthly activity at **201 streams**, while several 2024 months also approached **200 streams**.
### Visual 4; Listening Time(hours) by Device Type Over Time (Area Chart)
* Tablet users generated the highest total listening time, while Smart Speaker users generated the lowest
* The area chart also shows how listening time across device categories changes throughout the two-year period.
