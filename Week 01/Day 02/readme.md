# ⚽ Day-02; Fitness Tracking Analysis

## 📌 Business Overview & Objective; this analysis explores Fitness Tracking evaluation by Various Factors.

## 📊 Key KPIs; 
* **Total Fitness Logs:** 4k
* **Total Calories Burned:** 2M
* **Total Steps:** 36M
* **Average Sleep Hours:** 6.51

## 💡Key Insights;
### Visual 1: Calories Burned By Activity (Donut Chart)
* Gym Workout contributes the highest total recorded calorie expenditure at **267,878** calories, while Running contributes **216,873**. 
* The calorie distribution broadly follows the activity-log distribution, although the totals also depend on activity duration and intensity.
### Visual 2; Activity Participation (Funnel Chart)
* Gym Workout has the highest recorded participation with 534 logs, followed by Cycling with 524 and Swimming with 516. Running has the lowest participation at 467 logs.. 
* The relatively narrow difference between the activities suggests that users engage across a variety of fitness activities rather than concentrating heavily on one activity.
### Visual 3; Monthly Calories Burned (line chart)
* March recorded the highest monthly calorie expenditure at **155,130 calories**, while September recorded the lowest at **125,817**.
* The monthly pattern fluctuates rather than showing a consistent upward or downward trend.
### Visual 4; Monthly Steps Trend (Area Chart) 
* May recorded the highest monthly step volume at approximately **3.26 million** steps, while November recorded the lowest at approximately **2.60 million**.
* Step activity varies considerably by month, with several peaks during March, May, July and October.
